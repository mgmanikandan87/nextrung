# v0.4: mentors, admin, and guardrails on self-reported standing

Date: 2026-09-21. Decision owner: Mani. Built from the conversation "how do we know where a student really stands, and how does a mentor keep assigning what is best for them".

## The problem

Everything in v0.3 was self-reported. A student could paste any link as proof and the readiness ring filled. Self-serve learning has single-digit completion rates; the thing most tier-3 students lack is not information but one person who checks on them. So v0.4 adds two roles and makes "standing" something the system can see rather than something the student declares.

## Principles

1. Nothing counts because the student said it; things count because they showed it.
2. Guardrails calibrate, they do not police. A check is not an exam; nobody fails; retakes are allowed.
3. Consent first. A mentor sees nothing until the student links them (by code) or accepts a request. The student can remove a mentor at any time. A mentor sees plan, proofs, check results and self-ratings, never the email password or anything outside NextRung.
4. Mentor time is the scarce resource. Design for ten minutes per student per week, async: read flags, one note or one review. No chat.
5. The one thing that cannot be faked is a mentor looking at the work. Spend that once per student, at the point that matters (the monthly evidenced project).

## What was built

Four layers, cheapest first.

**Verified proofs** (`verify_url` in the Lambda). GitHub repositories are checked through the public API: exists, not a fork, commit count and how many distinct days the commits span, and whether the owner matches the GitHub username the student set under Settings. LeetCode profiles are read through the public GraphQL endpoint (solved counts, ownership by username). Other links are loaded: 200 with a known learning host is "seen", 401/403 is "seen, needs login", 404 or no response is "link broken". Labels: verified, seen (partial), link broken (unverified), checking (pending, GitHub rate limit). The label sits next to the link for the student, the mentor and on the public page. Verification never blocks saving.

**Calibration checks** (`data/checks/*.json`, 25 skills, 275 items). Six items per check, four applied and two basic, drawn at random, options shuffled per delivery; answers stay in the Lambda bundle and never reach the browser. The student predicts their score first. The result shows the score, the gap, and every wrong item with the right answer and a one-line explanation. One check per skill per 24 hours; attempts are counted and visible to the mentor.

**Consistency flags** (`flags_of`), computed from data already stored: inactive 14+ days, no proof in the first two weeks, three or more proofs added on one day, broken links, redo pending, overconfident (predicted at least two more than scored), mismatch (rated "can do alone" and scored under 50%). Flags sort the mentor's list.

**Mentor review**. Per student, a standing table over the route's skills: self-rating, check result with prediction, proof with label and facts. Actions: looks good / ask to redo (with a line the student sees; a redo proof stops counting until updated), confirm in person (creates a mentor-confirmed proof), make this the focus (moves the skill to the top of the student's Month 1), and notes. Every action emails the student when SES is configured.

**Admin**. Overview (accounts, active 7 days, mentors, links, proofs by label, checks), funnel by 7/30/all days, people list with role changes, mentor assignment (creates a pending request the student must allow), digest trigger.

## What was deliberately not built

- Proctoring, timers, or anti-cheat on checks. A student who pastes questions into a chatbot only cheats the calibration signal, and the mentor review catches the rest.
- Chat between mentor and student. Notes are one-way and emailed; keeps mentors from burning out.
- Automatic verification of certificates. Coursera and NPTEL pages load but ownership cannot be confirmed without their APIs; labelled "seen" and left to the mentor.
- Scores as a ranking. The check result is shown to the student and mentor only, never on the public page.
