# v0.2 usability review (21 Sep 2026)

Three simulated reviews of the v0.2 screens as seen on a phone (`v0.2-pagetext.json`): Priya (final-year ECE, tier-3, Tamil Nadu), Rohit (Mech 2025 pass-out, Kanpur, 8 months without a job), and an expert panel (consumer PO for first-time internet users, UX writer, tier-3 placement officer). Simulated personas are not real users; the 30-to-50-student test still has to happen. But the three converged, so v0.3 acts on the overlap.

## What all three said
- The home page leads with a thesis and statistics; a student wants "what should I do" in one line and a button. "Free. No course. No fees." must be the first thing.
- Vocabulary is for reviewers: standing, evidence, readiness, door state, fit rules, sprint, required/preferred, GCC, repo, deployed URL, base + fit. Replace with: my progress, proof, how ready, jobs growing/fewer, why this suits you, month 1, must have / good to have, MNC India offices, GitHub link, live link.
- Results show scoring arithmetic (+3/−1, "7 base + 8 fit") which reads as being graded; "shrinking door" as rank 1 contradicts the home page without a plain-line reconciliation.
- The Me page does six jobs at once (rings, 13 skills with forms, 3 sprints, employer table, share, delete); 0% rings and "0/10 at NTT DATA" feel like a rejection letter. Show the next one or three things first.
- Tables do not survive phones; job rows repeat the same skill; stat ids leak into prose; the path page is 3,000 words with the useful "is it realistic from your college" paragraph buried.
- Ask for sign-in after the student has seen Month 1, not before; say what is saved and that nothing goes to companies.
- Hindi/Tamil at least for home, quiz, results and the first plan steps. Mobile-number OTP is expected (deferred: Cognito SMS needs SNS spend approval).
- Placement officers need: sources dated on the first screen, the tier-3 paragraph surfaced, a drives-and-deadlines view, and no self-promotion push.

## v0.3 acts on
1. Home: one sentence, Start, free/no-course line, "N job ads checked <date>"; stats and chart below a "Why we built this" fold.
2. Global rename per the vocabulary audit; nav collapsed to Start / Paths / Jobs / Me; Sources and About in the footer.
3. Results: three cards (name, jobs trend in words, one why, one watch-out, one button); maths behind "See how we ranked this"; the rest as a plain list without numbers; a reconciliation line for a shrinking rank-1.
4. Plan preview before sign-in: Month 1 visible for the chosen path; then "Save my plan (free)".
5. Me split into Me / Plan / Jobs for me / Settings; one primary action per screen; Month 1 expanded, later months collapsed; "Add proof" opens one form at a time.
6. Cards instead of tables everywhere on the student side; deduped skills; stat ids stripped from prose; path page reordered with the tier paragraph and must-haves first, everything else collapsed.
7. Hindi for chrome, quiz, results and plan labels (content stays English for now); Tamil/Telugu/Kannada as contributions.
8. Anonymous funnel events (start, quiz done, path picked, plan viewed, signed in, proof added) so the student test can be measured.

## Deferred, with reasons
- Mobile OTP: Cognito SMS requires an SNS spending limit increase; email OTP for the test cohort.
- Drives-and-deadlines calendar: needs a verified source per drive; the government notifications in `data/jobs/government_psu.json` are the seed.
- Weekly nudge (email/WhatsApp): needs SES out of sandbox; design the message first.
- Batch view for placement officers: v0.4.

## v0.3 re-review (same personas, same day)
Both reviewers: the Start → results → plan preview → save loop now works; the expert panel gave a conditional go for a 30-to-50-student test once three fixes landed. Fixed before deploy: Month 1 reduced to three must-have items with hours per week (was the whole skill list at 85 hours); "This week" card leads the plan preview and the Me page; progress ring hidden until the first proof; route-trend pill removed from the Me header; Jobs for me sorted by match, then nearest region, then freshest, with "may be closed" on ads older than 60 days and an "unpaid or low pay" flag; pay rounded; "door" replaced by "route" in displayed text; Hindi "why we ask" strings; "Best for / Rarely" branch line instead of strikethrough. Still open (needs real users or more work): mobile-number OTP, Tamil/Telugu/Kannada, a drives-and-deadlines calendar, WhatsApp weekly nudge, city filter on Jobs.
