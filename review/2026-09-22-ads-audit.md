# Ads audit, 22 September 2026

105 fresher ads (96 companies) mapped to the 83-skill taxonomy; method and charts published as the "NextRung Ads Audit" page (claude.ai artifact); figures in `2026-09-22-ads-audit-data.json`.

Findings: workplace English is the most-asked skill (57 of 105 ads, 20%+ of ads in 8 of 9 routes) but was in only three plans. Semiconductor, EV and Defence plans described different jobs than the ads (Month 1 covered 0 to 12% of a typical ad). IT services was missing Testing and CI/CD (73% of its ads). Six taxonomy skills appear in no ad. 55% of ads are in the South, none in the East or Central India. 24 ads undated, 20 older than 60 days, pay parseable in 27.

Changes applied 23 September (v0.4.1):
- English added as required (weight 2) to AI-native, Defence, EV, Infrastructure, Semiconductor; preferred on Government/PSU.
- IT services: Testing and CI/CD required. Infrastructure: Project management required; STAAD and Primavera preferred. AI-native: REST APIs required; React, JS/TS, ML basics preferred; RAG, PostgreSQL, LLM evals demoted. EV: Quality/manufacturing (3) and Excel required; Embedded C, MATLAB preferred; CAN/LIN and EV powertrain removed from the plan (still in the two-year text). Semiconductor: LabVIEW/DAQ, Analog, PCB design required; RTL and DFT demoted/removed. Defence: Avionics required, GATE weight 2.
- Build now computes `demand` per path skill (share of the route's ads asking for it) and the plan orders Month 1 by weight × (1 + 2·demand); skill cards and route pages show "asked in N% of ads".
- Ingest skill: posting date required, ≤2 ads per company per route, regional minimums, `kind` tag per requirement line, pay parsed to lakh/yr.

Not done: splitting Semiconductor into a VLSI-design track and an electronics test/manufacturing track needs a new route record (plan, project, employers, fit rules) and should be written by the career-path-mapper skill against the stats, not improvised.
